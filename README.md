# Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes synthetic e-commerce sales data to transform transaction data into actionable business insights.

The analysis focuses on sales performance, product and category performance, geographic distribution, and revenue trends to support data-driven business decisions.

The project was developed using Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib and  Seaborn, nwith a focus on exploratory data analysis and business storytelling.

---

## Business Problem

The e-commerce company generates a large volume of sales data but lacks the tools and processes to transform it into meaningful business intelligence.

This limits its ability to optimize inventory, target marketing campaigns, identify sales trends, and determine the most promising markets for expansion.

The analysis aims to answer four key business questions:

- **What should we sell?** Identify the best-performing products.
- **Where should we focus?** Identify the categories and regions generating the highest revenue.
- **When should we act?** Analyze revenue trends and periods of high and low demand.
- **Where should we expand?** Identify geographic markets with stronger sales performance.

---

## Project Objectives

The main objective is to transform sales data into actionable insights that support data-driven decision-making.

The analysis seeks to:

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Analyze monthly revenue trends.
- Identify periods of high and low demand.
- Analyze the geographic distribution of sales.
- Identify potential opportunities for inventory optimization.
- Support marketing and expansion strategies.

---

## Dataset

The dataset used in this project is **synthetic and randomly generated for educational and portfolio purposes**.

The column names were also generated using a dictionary during the dataset creation process.

Because the dataset was generated in a structured format and did not require additional preprocessing, the project focuses directly on data exploration, visualization, and business analysis.

> **Note:** The data does not represent real customers, transactions, or companies.
